# Problem Collections

GenesisGeo is evaluated on several problem collections. They differ in size and in what kind of evidence they provide.

## IMO-AG-30

30 olympiad geometry problems from IMO 2000–2022, translated into the formal language. This is the original AlphaGeometry benchmark. It is small and prestigious — best treated as a flagship benchmark rather than a complete characterization of system behavior.

GenesisGeo solves **29/30** on this benchmark.

## IMO-95

95 problems from IMO competitions and shortlists. Provides broader coverage than IMO-AG-30 across more formalization styles and difficulty levels.

GenesisGeo solves **63/95** on this benchmark.

## HAGeo-409

409 hard geometry problems from USAMO, USATST, and international olympiads. This is the largest evaluation set and the most informative about practical coverage and generalization.

GenesisGeo solves **278/409** on this benchmark.

## JGEX-AG-231

An AlphaGeometry-era benchmark of shorter formal geometry problems. Useful for measuring solver breadth and engineering stability.

## Examples collection

A curated set for diagnosing the engine:

- simple theorem checks
- feature regression tests
- known configurations (orthocenter, Euler line, etc.)
- stress tests for specific proof behaviors

Theorem provers often fail in highly local ways. A broad public score can hide a broken rule or a regression in proof tracing. Curated examples expose those issues directly.
